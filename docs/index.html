About

Starry Sky Simulator plots stars from the Hipparcos dataset at their real positions in 3D space, with a Milky Way backdrop from NASA's Deep Star Maps. Unlike a planetarium app that shows the sky from Earth, it lets you fly anywhere within 1,000 light‑years of the Sun.

As you move, the stars and constellations shift and warp based on your position. From the menu you can toggle star labels, constellation lines and constellation labels.
